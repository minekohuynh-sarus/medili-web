    Food safety
    
  
    Six things you must not skip.
    
  
    Cold brew tastes good because of its long cold steep, and that is exactly why it needs stricter hygiene and cold-keeping than hot-brewed coffee.
    
  
    Cold brew is a low-acid food
    Hot-brewed coffee and cold brew have a pH of about 4.85–5.13, above the 4.6 threshold used to define acid foods. In a sealed, low-oxygen bottle kept warm, Clostridium botulinum bacteria can produce toxin. That is why cold brew bottles must be kept cold from steeping until the customer drinks them.
    Steep cold, keep below 5 °C
    Food safety guidance for cold brew without heat treatment: steep, store and serve at 5 °C or below. Guidance for commercial brewers: steep 16–20 hours in a refrigerator below 4.4 °C.
    Short shelf life without treatment
    The same guidance limits the date mark to at most 7 days from production for facilities without a HACCP plan. MeDili uses 7 days for chilled bottles. A longer shelf life requires microbiological testing over time, or high-pressure processing (HPP): one study found HPP at 600 MPa for 3 minutes cut pathogenic bacteria by more than 6 log and kept the product safe for 90 days, but it was not tested against C. botulinum spores.
    No nitrogen flushing, no canning on a whim
    In the US there have been recalls of canned cold brew, including nitro versions, because the process could allow C. botulinum to produce toxin, or because the canned low-acid food process had not been approved. No illnesses were reported, but it shows that sealed, low-oxygen packaging is a risk point.
    Clean water, tanks and filter bags
    Use drinking water that meets standards: tested RO water or bottled drinking water. The 304 stainless steel tank, food-grade cloth filter bags, bottles and caps are washed, sanitized and dried before every batch. Each batch has a record of times, temperatures and TDS, and 2 sample bottles are kept until expiry.
    Same-day chilled delivery
    Chilled bottles are only delivered within the city on the same day, in foam boxes with gel ice, reminding customers to refrigerate them as soon as they arrive. Never send chilled bottles to other provinces by standard delivery. To sell nationwide on marketplaces, use cold-steep bags: dry goods that customers steep at home.
    

    


    
  
    Paperwork to sell legally
    
  
    Six steps before listing Cold Brew MeDili on a marketplace. Reference information based on regulations in force in November 2026; check the original texts or ask the authorities before submitting documents.
    
  
    1. 01Business registration that covers food
      DSunfood Food Co., Ltd. is named as the organization responsible for the product. Check that food production and trading are listed on the registration certificate.
    2. 02Food safety eligibility certificate for the facility
      Mandatory for the production site (Article 11, Decree 15/2018), except for the cases in Article 12, such as facilities with valid HACCP, ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000. If you outsource production, choose a facility that already has this certificate. Businesses that only trade prepackaged food are not required to obtain it.
    3. 03Product testing
      Send samples to a designated or ISO 17025-accredited testing lab. The results report must be dated within 12 months of the submission date. The lab advises on the set of criteria: cold brew bottles are usually checked against QCVN 6-2:2010/BYT for non-alcoholic beverages; steeping bags are roasted ground coffee, with TCVN 5251:2015 as a reference.
    4. 04Product self-declaration
      Under Articles 4–5 of Decree 15/2018: a self-declaration on Form 01 with the test report; publish it on your own website or post it at your premises, and submit 1 copy to the agency designated by the provincial People's Committee. You may sell immediately after self-declaring. Decree 15/2018 applies because Decree 46/2026 has been suspended since April 6, 2026 under Resolution 15/2026. Check again when the amended Food Safety Law takes effect.
    5. 05A label with all mandatory content
      Under Decree 43/2017, amended by Decree 111/2021: product name, responsible organization, origin, quantity, production date, expiry date, ingredients, directions for use, storage and warnings. The back label in the Packaging section fills itself in for the batch.
    6. 06List on the marketplace
      Upload the self-declaration and documents to your store when the marketplace requires them for food and beverages. State the storage method and delivery area correctly. Each marketplace's rules change over time; check the seller center before listing.
