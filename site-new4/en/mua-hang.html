Pure, additive-free roasts from the Di Linh plateau. Choose the right coffee for your home or café, see the price per cup, then send your order to MeDili.

Buying single bags? Go to the MeDili Store, add to cart and send your order. The Buying Room helps cafés work out usage and wholesale prices.

MeDili Policies

  • 7-day returns: for manufacturing faults, packing faults, damage in transit or the wrong product delivered. Send your order code with photos or video of the fault; MeDili responds within 3 working days.
  • Support: advice on choosing coffee, grind size, brew ratio, basic recipes, menus, grinders and brewing equipment. The scope of support is agreed with each partner.
  • Delivery: nationwide. The fee and delivery time are given before the order is confirmed.
  • Wholesale prices: based on the total volume bought in the month. From 5 kg you get the tier 1 wholesale price. When you reach 20 kg in the month, the difference is credited to your next order.
  • Our commitment: pure coffee, matching the declared ingredients, unadulterated, with no flavorings or preservatives added to the pure-roast lines.
