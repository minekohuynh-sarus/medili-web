Screening and sorting: screen sizes and defect standards

Screen sizing helps the beans in a batch roast evenly. Sorting removes defective beans before the heat spreads their smell through the whole batch.

Check a sample before accepting a lot
  • Sample: at least 350 g, taken from many points in the lot.
  • Sorting table: matte black surface, 4000 K light, 1,200 lux.
  • Moisture: 10–12% per SCA; TCVN allows at most 12.5%.
  • Specialty: no category 1 defects and at most 5 category 2 defects in 350 g. This standard is for Washed Arabica, so for Robusta use it only as a reference.
  • Density: pour freely into a 1 L tube, level it off and weigh. For example 690 g/L. Record it to track the lot.

    Safety limits

    • Always keep someone at the machine. Over-roasted beans can catch fire in the drum.
    • Clean out chaff and the chimney on schedule. Oily dust and chaff are common causes of fire.
    • Gas and smoke: the machine must vent smoke outside, the room must be ventilated, and there must be a fire extinguisher.
    • Dark roasts: drop and cool immediately, because the temperature climbs fast at the end of the batch.
