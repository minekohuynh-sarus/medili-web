Chapter 01 · Professional coffee roaster

From Origin Robusta roots.
To the art of roasting.

MeDili is a coffee roaster on the Di Linh plateau that chose Origin Robusta Di Linh as its core bean. The growing area has been cultivated since 2010, and the roastery is in Thôn 11, Di Linh commune. MeDili roasts coffee for homes, cafés, restaurants and hotels.
